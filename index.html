    Keys & tips
    • T toggles the selected plan on/off, a fast way to compare with what's there now.
    • M measures distances; double-click ends a line, Esc clears.
    • Multiply blending makes white paper see-through. White → transparent also works on dark photos.
    • The basemap switcher (top-left) has the Warsaw 2021 5 cm photomap and cadastral parcels, which help to check alignment.
